_New - Now available once again the transit for pipelaying and pipelaser alignment_  
**[The David White LTU ( Line Transfer Unit ) with laser plummet](uploads/3/1/7/6/3176472/davidwhite-_lt8300ltulp_brochure.pdf)**  
  
Almost indispensable in obtaining line and invert in pipe laying operations, our Line Transfer Unit can be used with pipe aligning lasers or as transit-levels. The LTU allows fast setup and double-checking of laser's grade, and can also be used for leveling and grading work.
